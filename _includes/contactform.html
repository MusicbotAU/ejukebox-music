{% comment %} The enquiry form. Posts as JSON to the Azure Function, which sends the mail through Microsoft Graph from a mailbox in the eJukebox tenant. NO EMAIL ADDRESS APPEARS ANYWHERE IN THIS MARKUP, deliberately. Publishing one is what attracts the scrapers, and Marc does not want that on the child sites. If the endpoint ever fails, the form keeps what the visitor typed and offers a retry - it never falls back to a mailto, because that would put an address in the page source, which is the thing we are avoiding. Params: site - "ejukebox.tv" or "ejukebox.music". Tells the function which site the enquiry came from, and titles the email. heading - optional h2 text. Omit to render the form with no heading, for embedding under a heading the page already has. intro - optional paragraph above the fields. cta - submit button label.
