{% comment %} THE ROOM - one venue, drawn once, here. This file is rendered TWICE on the hero, once inside .rooms__layer--cold and once inside .rooms__layer--warm. Because both renders come out of this single file, the geometry is provably identical: the only things that differ are the five custom properties the layer sets (--ink, --ink-2, --glassc, --edge and --floor) and the lean the stylesheet applies to .rm-f on the warm layer. Nothing in here carries an id, so it can be duplicated safely. Nothing on .rm-f or .rm-glass carries a transform attribute either, because a CSS transform would replace it - every figure is drawn at its final position. The light in here comes from lamps hanging over the tables, never from a screen. No frame, no bezel: sound has neither. {% endcomment %}
