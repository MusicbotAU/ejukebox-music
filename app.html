The free app

Every radio station in Australia and New Zealand. One tap, and the best sound it broadcasts.

eJukebox Music is a radio app for your phone and your car. Free, no account, no advertising of ours. Every station we can find a public stream for, the commercial networks and the ABC and RNZ and the little community stations, and our own eJukebox channels at the top.

What it does

A radio. A good one.

Every station

All of them, checked daily

More than eight hundred Australian and New Zealand stations, sorted by where you are. The list rebuilds itself every night, so a station that moves its stream comes back on its own and a dead one drops out.

The best sound

The highest quality each station puts out

Most stations broadcast several streams. The app picks the one that sounds best, marks it HD, and lets you pick a lighter one from the STREAM row if you are watching your data.

In the car

Locked, in your pocket, on the wheel

It keeps playing with the screen off. Next and previous on the steering wheel, the lock screen or your earbuds step through your favourites in the order you put them.

Record

Like a cassette, at full quality

Press Record and the app keeps what is playing until you stop it or the time you set runs out. Recordings play back like any station, phone locked, for as long as they run. For your own listening.

Ratings

Stars from real listeners

Rate a station one to five and everyone sees the average within a minute. Sort by top rated when you want to know what people in your part of the country actually listen to.

Yours

Favourites, hidden, and a backup

Keep the ones you love at the top, tuck away the ones you never want to see, and it all follows you to a new phone. No account needed - it rides on the phone you already own.

Why we made it

A venue music company made a radio app. Here is the connection.

Getting people listening.

eJukebox is not a streaming company. Everything we do is about getting people in a room to listen to music, and to stay for it. In a pub, a club or a business that means eJukebox playing the room, and the people in it requesting their own songs from their phones and taking control of the music at their favourite venue.

The app is the same idea, outside the venue. Every station you could want, the best version of each, and you in charge of what plays. Same people, same reason, different room.

The sound

Why it sounds better than the station's own app.

A station's own app usually plays one stream, and it is not always the good one. eJukebox Music knows every stream a station puts out, what codec it uses and how many kilobits it carries, and it ranks them by how they actually sound rather than by the number. A modern AAC stream at a lower number beats an old MP3 at a higher one, so that is the one you get.

The stations stay as they were made, ads and all. We change nothing on the way through and we never sit in the middle of the stream. Your phone talks to the broadcaster directly.
